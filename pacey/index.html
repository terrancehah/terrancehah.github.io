Questions before you start

What is Pacey?

Pacey is a race-readiness companion. It brings Garmin training and recovery data, your race goal and the course together to explain what is working, what needs attention and how to prepare for your next race.

Do I need a Garmin account?

Connect your Garmin account to use your own training data, or explore the demo with sample data. Pacey uses python-garminconnect, an open-source project on GitHub, to load your Garmin data.

How does race readiness relate to my goal time?

Race readiness compares your current fitness and recent activities with the goal time you set. It constantly highlights your strengths, the areas to develop and what to focus on before race day.

How does the training plan reach Garmin?

Pacey shows a training block of up to 26 weeks. You can review the suggested workouts, then sync the current calendar week’s suggested workouts to your Garmin watch.

Can I upload my own race course for analysis?

Yes. You can upload a GPX file to view the route and elevation profile. The Pacey coach will study the route, consider the climbs and where they occur when explaining race preparation.

How is Pacey different from Garmin Training Readiness?

Garmin Training Readiness helps you judge how ready you are to train today. Pacey compares your recent running fitness with the race goal time you choose, highlights areas to develop and uses the biggest gap to shape your training plan. Pacey is an independent product, not an official Garmin service.

Does Pacey predict my marathon finish time?

No. Pacey assesses your readiness against the goal time you set; it does not predict a finish time or guarantee a race result. Your readiness review explains the training evidence and what to work on before race day.
