Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a Notion account?

Yes. You need a Notion account to duplicate the template into your workspace and use it.

What is included in the template?

A class calendar, individual lesson-plan pages, a list of past lessons, sharing through Notion, and customisable cover pictures.

How do I add the template to my workspace?

After purchase, open the template link you receive and duplicate it into your own Notion workspace.

Can I use it on desktop and mobile?

Yes. The template can be used in both the desktop and mobile versions of Notion.

Can I adapt it to my teaching style?

Yes. Once duplicated, you can edit lesson content, objectives, topics, remarks and cover pictures in your own copy.

Can I plan lessons with colleagues?

Yes. Use Notion's sharing controls to invite colleagues and give them the appropriate access to work on the syllabus together.

Is this a subscription?

No. The template is a one-time purchase. It is used within your own Notion workspace.
