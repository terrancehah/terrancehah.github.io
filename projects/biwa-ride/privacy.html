Privacy notice

This page describes what the Lake Biwa Ride Companion stores and shares. It is a small private tool for three riders — there are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What is stored

The shared trip (itinerary, places, manual checklist ticks and notes) is stored in a dedicated Redis database so all three phones see the same state. It is public shared state for this trip — no accounts and no invite token — served to browsers with private, no-store headers and kept with no expiry.

Your location

If you tap “use my location”, your browser takes a position that stays on your device for the rest of the session and centres every Nearby shortcut search you open — the coordinates travel to Google Maps inside those links only. The fix is never sent to our server, never stored beyond the session and never shared with the other riders.

Google services

Map previews, road-route geometry, terrain data and place details come from Google Maps Platform. When you view a map or request a route, the stop coordinates are sent to Google to compute the preview. Route, elevation and place details are shown transiently and are not stored with the trip — the only provider data kept server-side is the small set Google's caching rules allow: a resolved Google place ID (no expiry) and place coordinates (30 days at most). Photos, opening hours, ratings and photo links are never stored. Google's own privacy policy applies to that traffic.

Logs and tokens

There are no accounts and no access tokens. Provider keys live server-side — except the Maps JavaScript key, which is deliberately public but locked by HTTP-referrer restrictions to this site. None are written to logs or sent anywhere but Google's own endpoints. This site's referrer policy is strict-origin-when-cross-origin: third-party sites you open from links (including Google Maps, whose script needs an origin referrer for its restricted key) may see that you came from this site.
