How to capture your photos

Record a short side-view clip of yourself running, then screenshot these four key moments of your stride. Keep the whole body in frame and the camera roughly level with your hips.

1

Initial Ground Contact

The lead foot first touches down, reaching ahead of the body.

2

Full Ground Contact

Body weight passes directly over the planted foot.

3

Toe-Off

Push-off, when the trailing ankle reaches farthest behind you.

4

Swing (Thigh Raised)

The leg swings through with the thigh lifted toward the top.
